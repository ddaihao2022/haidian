# Jing-Zhang Sport & Health Vitality Belt

## Design Basis and Source Register
This formal proposal takes the pre-qualification announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt international urban-design call as its primary basis, together with the maintainer-registered provisional boundary, key areas, enums, metrics and source register under `brief/site-package/`. All design judgments are decomposed into traceable sources, recalculable metrics, verifiable layers and human-reviewable assumptions. The package reaches the urban-design depth of a regulatory-detailed-plan and an implementation-plan synthesis.

## Three-Level Scope Framework
The work is organized in three levels: a 43.6 km² research scope, an 11.4 km² overall design scope around the Jing-Zhang heritage park, and a 368.4 ha detailed-design scope across three key areas. The concept, "Jing-Zhang Sport & Health Vitality Belt", uses the heritage park as the public-space spine, three key areas as anchors, and a sport-health loop as the connector.

## Key-Area Detailed Design
Sport Park Core is positioned as the stadium-and-park node emphasizing events and active recreation. Health Slow Belt focuses on exercise trails, rehab gardens and non-motorized priority. Community Fitness Node centers on neighborhood gyms, plazas and sports booking.

## AI Scenarios and Talent Personas
Ten AI scenario cards and five user personas are mapped to concrete layers and the compliance matrix, covering sports booking, crowd-safety sandbox, night-wayfinding, health-route APP, community feedback wall, open-source workshop booking, talent matchmaking, edge-compute stations, culture-route APP and a campus-life route.

## Land Use, Building Scale and Renewal
Land use follows the national land-classification guide; building height, massing and retain-renovate-demolish classification are proposed as methods with official conditions pending. All scale metrics are recalculated from GeoJSON layers in `metrics.json`.

## Mobility, Rail, Municipal and Public Services
The plan addresses station integration, road micro-circulation, slow-mobility gaps, green transport, parking and non-motorized storage, with municipal and new-infrastructure strategies for distributed energy and edge compute.

## Blue-Green, Public Space and Townscape
A north-south, east-west connected blue-green system is built around the Jing-Zhang heritage park and the Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe corridors, integrating slow loops, public interaction and public display.

## Renewal Projects, Policy and Phasing
Six renewal projects (SP-01..SP-06) are listed with dependencies and evidence references, sequenced into near-term pilots, mid-term renewal and long-term governance, clearly separating the 100-day call period from implementation phasing.

## Metrics, Recalculation and Compliance
Core metrics (scope area, key-area area, Sport venues 56, Health trails 14.2 km, Public-space ratio 9.4%, renewal count) are recalculated from geometry. The compliance matrix maps every announcement and taskbook task to sections, layers, metrics, drawings, HTML, sources, assumptions and self-check items.

## Risk, Copyright and Compliance
This package does not claim official approval, finalized regulatory control, land ownership or guaranteed implementation. All assets declare sources and licenses in `sources.json` and `report/copyright_statement.md`. The HTML pages load no remote scripts, tiles, fonts, iframes or APIs.
