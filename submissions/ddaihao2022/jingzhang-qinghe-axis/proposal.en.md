# Jing-Zhang Open Belt - Qinghe Blue-Green Smart Interaction Axis

## Design Basis and Source Register
This formal proposal takes the pre-qualification announcement for the Centennial Jing-Zhang AI Innovation Belt international urban-design call as its primary basis, together with the maintainer-registered provisional boundary, key areas, enums, metrics and source register under `brief/site-package/`. All design judgments are decomposed into traceable sources, recalculable metrics, verifiable layers and human-reviewable assumptions. The package reaches the urban-design depth of a regulatory-detailed-plan and an implementation-plan synthesis.

## Three-Level Scope Framework
The work is organized in three levels: a 43.6 km² research scope, an 11.4 km² overall design scope around the Jing-Zhang heritage park, and a 368.4 ha detailed-design scope across three key areas. The overall concept, "Qinghe Blue-Green Smart Interaction Axis", uses the heritage park as the public-space spine, the three key areas as innovation anchors, and a blue-green slow-mobility composite loop as the connector.

## Key-Area Detailed Design
Zhongzhiyuan is positioned as a garden-type full-stack autonomy block emphasizing the Qinghe frontage, standards governance showcase and low-carbon compute experience. The AI Origin Community focuses on near-campus transfer, open-source collaboration and a talent zone. Dazhongsi is an urban intelligent-economy block centered on transit-oriented integration, walkable quadrants and international roadshows.

## AI Scenarios and Talent Personas
Ten AI scenario cards and five user personas are mapped to concrete layers and the compliance matrix, covering open-source release, safety-governance sandbox, edge-compute stations, AI walk navigation, international roadshow lobby, Qinghe low-carbon corridor, near-campus transfer street, data-element lounge, AI life-service street and a global AI activity-week route.

## Land Use, Building Scale and Renewal
Land use follows the national land-classification guide; building height, massing and retain-renovate-demolish classification are proposed as methods with official conditions pending. All scale metrics are recalculated from GeoJSON layers in `metrics.json`.

## Mobility, Rail, Municipal and Public Services
The plan addresses station integration, road micro-circulation, slow-mobility gaps, green transport, parking and non-motorized storage, with municipal and new-infrastructure strategies for distributed energy and edge compute.

## Blue-Green, Public Space and Townscape
A north-south, east-west connected blue-green system is built around the Jing-Zhang heritage park and the Qinghe and Xiaoyuehe corridors, integrating slow loops, sports, innovation interaction and public display.

## Renewal Projects, Policy and Phasing
Six renewal projects (JZ-01..JZ-06) are listed with dependencies and evidence references, sequenced into near-term pilots, mid-term renewal and long-term governance, clearly separating the 100-day call period from implementation phasing.

## Metrics, Recalculation and Compliance
Core metrics (scope area, key-area area, green and public-space ratios, building footprint, renewal count) are recalculated from geometry. The compliance matrix maps every announcement and taskbook task to sections, layers, metrics, drawings, HTML, sources, assumptions and self-check items.

## Risk, Copyright and Compliance
This package does not claim official approval, finalized regulatory control, land ownership or guaranteed implementation. All assets declare sources and licenses in `sources.json` and `report/copyright_statement.md`. The HTML pages load no remote scripts, tiles, fonts, iframes or APIs.

## Global Case Benchmarking

This proposal uses "translating a linear heritage corridor into a continuous public-life axis" as its method spine and benchmarks six real international cases to extract actionable local lessons rather than slogan-style analogies.

| Case | Type & Location | Real Lesson | Borrowed by this package |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| High Line | Elevated rail-park, New York | An abandoned freight viaduct became a continuous linear park driving renewal and slow-life | Treat the Jing-Zhang railway heritage as the "belt" public-space spine, not an isolated attraction |
| Cheonggyecheon | Daylit urban stream, Seoul | A buried stream reopened with continuous slow-mobility and night vitality, remaking the city core | Qinghe blue-green corridor: stream opening, slow-mobility and night lighting as one system |
| Seoullo 7017 | Station-cap overpass, Seoul | A station overpass turned into a sky-garden connector mending a expressway-split district | Dazhongsi / Wudaokou quadrant walkability and station-city slow-mobility mending |
| Route der Industriekultur / Emscher Park | Industrial heritage corridor, Ruhr, Germany | A unified signage system links coal/steel sites into a cyclable heritage route with pilgrimage landmarks | Belt-and-nodes heritage-corridor signage, honor landmarks and an annual activity route |
| The 606 / Bloomingdale Trail | Elevated rail-to-greenway, Chicago | A neighborhood-scale greenway carrying daily life and inclusive activities | Jing-Zhang Heritage Park slow loop: community inclusion and tiered activities |
| Zollverein UNESCO Site | Industrial heritage + design school, Essen | A heritage site hosts design education, innovation incubation and public showcase | Zhongzhiyuan: heritage frontage hosting standards-governance showcase and low-carbon exchange |

All borrowings are written as "concept suggestion / reference scheme" and must not be presented as confirmed government events or implementation; formal regulatory and ownership conditions remain required before landing.

## Logo and Visual Identity (VI)

The naming concept is "Jing-Zhang Smart Pulse / 京张智脉": "Smart Pulse" refers both to Zhan Tianyou's century-old Jing-Zhang railway "artery" and to the AI-era data pulse, consistent with the master spine "Jing-Zhang Smart-Pulse Symbiosis Belt". The logo juxtaposes two lines — a rail line (heritage) and a data-flow line (AI) — extending from Tsinghuayuan Station toward Qinghe, converging into a green loop at the Qinghe frontage, symbolizing the blue-green slow-mobility composite loop.

Color system (for signage, boards and HTML; hex given for reproduction):
- Jing-Zhang Pulse Blue `#1A6FB5` (heritage rail & tech base)
- Qinghe Green `#2E9E6B` (blue-green slow-mobility loop)
- Origin Orange `#E8743B` (Beijing AI Origin Community & talent)
- Dazhong Gold `#C9A227` (Dazhongsi international exchange)
- Ink Grey `#2B2F33` (text & signage base)

Typography: Source Han Sans for CJK signage, Inter / Roboto for Latin, JetBrains Mono for node codes (e.g. JZ-01). Wayfinding uses a three-tier code — belt (axial mileage markers), three cores (key-area colors: blue / orange / gold), and nodes (scenario pictograms) — with parallel CN/EN text to satisfy the v2 bilingual requirement.

## Implementation Matrix and Test Scenarios

Implementation is phased, with a clear boundary between lightweight launches and content pending formal conditions.

| Phase | Time | Focus | Key dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 Pilot | 0–12 mo | JZ-01 slow-mobility gap mending, Origin Community open-source hall, bilingual signage & AR | Road redlines, under-bridge space, event permits |
| Phase 2 Renewal | 1–3 yr | JZ-02 Zhongzhiyuan Qinghe frontage, JZ-04 Dazhongsi quadrants, edge-compute nodes | Official regulatory plan, river blue-line, municipal pipelines |
| Phase 3 Governance | 3–5 yr | JZ-06 Global AI Activity Week normalization, data-trust ops, honor system | Ownership, funding, approvals & operating entity |

Test & verification scenarios (per the taskbook's "no fewer than 3 industry test scenarios"):
- TS-01 AI walk-navigation gap identification: deploy explainable signage + low-intrusion sensors in the Jing-Zhang Heritage Park vitality belt; expected to identify ≥3 slow-mobility gaps and accessibility deficits via aggregate statistics only, no personal tracking.
- TS-02 Transit-interchange crowding prediction: run a crowd model at Dazhongsi and Wudaokou; expected accurate peak prediction, human-in-the-loop alerts, aggregated de-identified data within privacy bounds.
- TS-03 Edge-compute station low-carbon verification: baseline energy and carbon for an edge-compute station; expected an auditable load–carbon curve open to the public.

## Landmarks and Honor System

Two physical and one digital landmark are proposed, all emphasizing cleared licensing and public participation.
- Tsinghuayuan Station Memory Pavilion (physical): takes the original Jing-Zhang railway Tsinghuayuan Station as the heritage anchor, with readable historical exhibits and an AR overlay (historic trains against today's axis), serving as the cultural starting point of the belt.
- Open-Source Contribution Wall (digital): a public code-wall at the Beijing AI Origin Community honoring open-source contributors, with all cases and marks cleared.
- Jing-Zhang Smart-Pulse Honor Landmark (physical + digital): a yearly rotating honor marker at the Qinghe-frontage midpoint publishing annual Global AI Activity Week outcomes, linked to an online honor archive.

## Cultural Narrative and International Communication

Narrative spine: "From Zhan Tianyou's railway to today's smart pulse — one axis connecting a century of industrial heritage with AI future life." The narrative strings together Jing-Zhang railway history, Zhongguancun innovation culture and AI new culture into a walkable, communicable story line.

Real channels: the Global AI Activity Week route (a walkable experience from heritage culture → open-source community → industry showcase → international roadshow), the Dazhongsi international roadshow lobby, open-source community releases, bilingual signage & AR, academic and media collaboration with Tsinghua / Zhongguancun, and bilingual social media. All brands, images and corporate marks must be license-cleared; unauthorized content is never written into formal deliverables.

## Annual Activity System and Long-term Operations

Activity calendar (clear frequency and accountability):
- Q1 Jing-Zhang heritage walk + open-source release season (community co-op)
- Q2 Global AI Activity Week (annual flagship, June; Dazhongsi roadshow + Origin Community release)
- Q3 Qinghe low-carbon innovation festival (river & blue-green public activities)
- Q4 Standards-governance workshop + annual honor release

Operations & sustainability matrix: operating entity is a community co-op + data trustee; funding from event sponsorship, lightweight service fees and government procurement, prioritizing lightweight facilities, operations and service platforms over heavy-asset commitments; risks include event safety, license clearing, over-commercialization and data privacy, all recorded in assumptions and the risk section, never as government implementation promises.

## Accessibility and Privacy Protection

Accessibility follows WCAG 2.1 AA: bilingual signage meets contrast ratios, tactile and voice guides are provided, public space has accessible slow-mobility ramps and wheelchair-reachable nodes, and key areas and activity routes are verified for accessible connectivity.

Priority groups: older adults (nearby retirees), children (university families), persons with disabilities, low-income commuters, and international visitors (language barrier). Targeted measures include tiered activities that avoid disturbing residents, bilingual/icon signage, and low-intrusion sensors that aggregate only.

Data & privacy follow the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) and GDPR principles: data minimization, open sources, explainability and human review; walk navigation collects no personal trajectories and activity data is aggregated only; compute and data-element services require separate authorization; resident profiles are never used for commercial recommendation. All AI governance suggestions neither replace planning approval, nor output unauthorized personal profiles, nor claim official implementation commitments.
